====================================================================================================
HONEST CONFOUNDS
====================================================================================================
  - No clean EVIDENCE/ADVOCACY split. Old Bailey markup does not reliably role-tag witness testimony vs
    defence/prosecution framing (persName type=witnessName is largely absent), so whole-trial character
    is scored and evidence is CONTROLLED (offence + word count + n persons), not removed. Character still
    partly reflects how strong the evidence reads, so any residual character effect is an UPPER bound on
    rhetoric, not a clean estimate.
  - The account is a published clerk/shorthand REPORT, not a transcript; early trials are terse summaries,
    later ones near-verbatim. That composition change is itself the temporal signal AND a confound with era.
  - The 8-axis instrument is web/periodical-tuned; 17-19thC legal English is out of its training domain.
  - OCR quality improves over the span; word count as an evidence proxy is coarse.
  - guilty base rate is high; not-guilty is the minority class, so acquittal signal is the harder half.
