# Next steps: three lanes (governing plan after the v83 stop)

Decided after the effect side programme reached its honest boundary (`RESULT_POWER.md`, the v83 boundary
statement). The remaining uncertainty about the person by content coupling is **causal**, so more observational
evidence does not reduce it. The governing principle: the weapon is built and frozen; the next move is to find
someone to fire it, not to dig for a softer target. Doing less now increases credibility.

## Lane 1 (primary): take the severe test to a partner, the only lane that adjudicates

`ASHLAR_SEVERE_TEST_PROTOCOL_v1.0.md` is frozen and fundable. Put it to Fred Conrad (call 14 Sept), UCL
(Griffin), Sussex, a research house (Savanta or similar), or a funder. The proposition is deliberately
falsifying: we froze a test that could seriously hurt our own theory; here is the design, the analysis, the
sample size and the rule that decides it in advance; help us run it. Only a powered randomised experiment with
independently measured personality and experimentally varied message geometry can adjudicate the large causal
interpretation.

## Lane 2: cheap feasibility, only if a low cost signal is wanted, with a hard ceiling

A small ChangeMyView slice (not Wikipedia; CMV is the best construct fit because delta is an actual change of
view). Before anything expensive, ask the pre interaction questions:

- is there sufficient variation in content geometry, in the outcome, and in person specific response slopes to
  make a pre specified frozen interaction detectable at all?
- practical triage: if there is no content signal and no credible between person heterogeneity, stop.

Note the sharpening: a zero global character main effect does **not** logically rule out heterogeneous effects
that cancel across groups, so absence of a main effect is not an absolute veto; the real gate is detectable
variation for a pre specified interaction. Hard time and compute ceiling on this look. No giant ingest, no D8
reconstruction exercise, no "maybe one more corpus".

## Lane 3 (parked): the cross system observational idea, supporting evidence only

The same frozen operator predicting decisions across unrelated human systems (Wikimedia review, CMV, GitHub,
moderation) with no per domain retraining would be strong convergent evidence for the unification claim. Keep
parked; pursue only if resources permit; always label it supporting observational evidence, never the causal
test. FlaggedRevisions specifically is a poor construct fit (gatekeeping and verifiability dominate, the
setting is engineered to suppress the personality interaction, and any content signal is likely a main effect
from neutral point of view policy rather than the person by content interaction), so it is not the flagship.

## What we do not do

Do not start a large observational build now. That would reverse the deliberate stop and drift into the
post hoc correlation hunting the boundary statement rules out. The missing evidence has a specific causal
form; substitute nothing weaker for it.
