# The manner pole's own nomological net: the symmetric double dissociation

*DYNAMICS-WEB series, 3 September 2026. Script `manner_mirror_test.py`. The matter reductions showed matter
occupies the substance subspaces. This is the symmetric test, and its predictions, including the dark cells,
were written into the script before the numbers were read: if the geometry is structured rather than a
single quality axis, the manner pole must occupy the complementary territory, dark where matter is bright,
bright on affective and social constructs. 77,182 held `cc_v3.reddit_wide` comments (14,461 with a Grice
judgement). matter = mean z(rigour, depth); manner = mean z(affect, register). All external measures are
surface or lexicon, independent of the character scorer.*

## Reading

The manner pole occupies its own theoretically predicted territory. It is involved and affective production,
the mirror image of Biber's informational pole, loading positively on the social and emotional lexicon
(pronouns +0.21, emotion words +0.15, intensifiers +0.13) exactly where matter loads negatively, and it is
dark or negative on complexity, informativeness and cohesion. The positive identity lives in the affect
axis; register is near neutral on these measures, a refinement of where manner's signal sits. Together with
the matter reductions this is a double dissociation across the two poles, pre registered on all five cells:
the strong evidence that the axis is a structured geometry with two theoretically distinct ends, not a single
quality or verbosity dimension. Bounds: English, one reader lineage, Reddit register (which floors the
emoji and caps features); the affective lexicon is a crude proxy for validated affect instruments, so the
magnitudes are a lower bound on the manner cell.
