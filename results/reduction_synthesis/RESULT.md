# The four reductions together: a nomological net for the matter against manner axis

*DYNAMICS-WEB series, 3 September 2026. A synthesis of the four framework reductions run against the eight
axis character instrument, all on held scores, analysis only. Each is written up in full in its own
directory; this document reads them as one pattern. The claim it supports, and the one it stops short of, both stated plainly. The reductions do not show that
matter against manner is a generic linguistic quality or complexity axis. Across independent theoretical
frameworks it preferentially recovers the constructs occupying their substantive or theoretically adjacent
subspaces while failing to recover orthogonal ones. That pattern of convergence and discrimination is more
consistent with a structured latent geometry than with a single undifferentiated verbosity or quality
factor. It does not by itself establish that the geometry is generative, that it is the thing which produces
the text rather than a structured description of it, and that harder question is left open here.*

## Canonical summary

> Across four independent linguistic and communication frameworks, the matter against manner geometry
> demonstrates convergent and discriminant validity. Matter preferentially recovers constructs associated
> with informational substance, complexity, argument quality and informativeness, while remaining weak or
> null on cohesion and formal Manner. Conversely, the manner pole shows the predicted inverse relationship
> with matter related constructs and positive associations with involved and affective linguistic
> production. The resulting double dissociation is inconsistent with a simple verbosity, readability or
> generic quality explanation, although it does not by itself establish that the geometry is a generative
> latent substrate.

This paragraph is the agreed wording for the reduction programme; the sections below are its evidence.

## The four results on one axis

| Framework | Its own primary contrast | What the matter axis recovers | Strength |
|---|---|---|---|
| Biber (1988) register dimensions | involved versus informational production (Dimension 1) | the informational pole | genre unit r −0.60; within unit, reliability corrected, −0.17 |
| Coh-Metrix / TAALES / TAACO reading science | text complexity and sophistication, and, separately, cohesion | the complexity and sophistication constructs | disattenuated within room 0.24 to 0.45 (grade level, sentence and word length, lexical density) |
| Grice cooperative principle | quantity, quality, relation, manner | the quantity and relation maxims | length controlled partial r 0.12 to 0.18 |

## What it does NOT recover, which is the load bearing half

An instrument that measured length or a generic quality halo would light up on every linguistic index it met.
This one is dark, or reversed, exactly where each framework places the subspace orthogonal to substance:

- **Coh-Metrix cohesion.** Connective density is −0.01 to −0.04 and adjacent sentence overlap +0.10, against
  0.24 to 0.45 for the complexity constructs. The axis reads how dense a text is, not how connected.
- **Grice manner.** The clarity and brevity maxim is a clean null for every axis (length controlled partial
  +0.04), while quantity and relation survive. The axis reads informativeness, not clarity of delivery.
- **ELM peripheral cue.** Raw affect correlates with argument quality *negatively* (−0.087), the opposite
  sign to the central pair, which is discriminant validity in the predicted direction rather than a weak
  positive.

## Why the null cells matter more than the positive ones

The four positive cells share a length and complexity component, and it is real and large: matter correlates
with log comment length at +0.38, so part of what it shares with Biber's informational pole, the Coh-Metrix
complexity indices and the Grice quantity maxim is that substantive writing is longer and denser. That is
why the discriminant evidence carries the argument. A verbosity or length artefact would inflate every
linguistic correlation together; it would not leave Grice's manner maxim and Coh-Metrix cohesion at zero
while quantity, relation, complexity and the ELM central route stand. The selective pattern, substance
subspaces load and expression subspaces do not, is what a length artefact cannot produce, and the Grice
length control makes that explicit: the manner maxim dies under it and the relation maxim does not.

## The symmetric test: the manner pole occupies the complementary territory

The strongest single check that this is a structured geometry and not one quality axis is the mirror of
the matter net, and its predictions were pre registered including the dark cells. The manner pole (affect
and register) is dark or negative exactly where matter is bright, complexity −0.15 against matter +0.22,
the Grice quantity and relation maxims −0.14 against matter +0.34, and it is bright on the affective and
social lexicon where matter is negative: first and second person pronouns +0.21 against matter −0.17,
emotion words +0.15 against −0.18, intensifiers +0.13 against −0.08. Cohesion and the Grice manner maxim
are dark for both. Every one of the five cells fell in the predicted direction. That is a double
dissociation across the two poles, and it is the manner pole recovering Biber's involved production, the
mirror of the informational pole matter recovers. The positive identity lives in the affect axis; register
is near neutral on these measures. Full result in `../reduction_manner_mirror/`.

## The claim at its proper tier, and the two lines it does not cross

The matter against manner axis behaves as an **informational substance** dimension with a coherent
nomological net: it is Biber's informational pole, the Coh-Metrix complexity constructs, the ELM central
route, and the Grice quantity and relation maxims, and it is not Coh-Metrix cohesion, the Grice manner
maxim, or the ELM peripheral cue. Four independent traditions, each recovered in the subspace it predicts
and absent in the subspace it treats as orthogonal. The positive correlations establish convergence; the
selective pattern establishes structure.

Two distinctions are held deliberately, because the strong version of each would be an overclaim. First,
this is offered as a nomological *net*, the interpretation the pattern invites, not as *nomological validity
demonstrated*, which is a formal psychometric claim that would need a pre specified construct network, an
error model on each external measure and a fitted structural test rather than a table of correlations; the
former is compelling on the evidence here, the latter is not yet earned. Second, and more fundamentally,
structure is not generativity. A structured latent geometry that intersects four frameworks in their
substantive subspaces is a strong description of the character signal; it does not show the geometry is what
produces the writing. That is the hard question the competitor test and the equation of state circle, and
nothing here settles it. The reductions make the description hard to dismiss as a verbosity artefact; they
do not make it a mechanism.

Bounds held in the open. All four are English and, except the cross reader legs elsewhere, one reader
lineage. The convergent correlations are modest once length is controlled (Grice) or read within the unit
(Biber, −0.17), so the strength of the net is in its shape, which cells are populated and which are empty,
not in any single coefficient. Fleeson (the trait against situation split, 51.6 against 48.4) is a fifth
reduction of a different kind, a variance form rather than a construct correlation, and is reported with
the coupling rather than here. The manner pole's own nomological net, the frameworks that should populate
where this axis is dark, is the symmetric test still owed.
