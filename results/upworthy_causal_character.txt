  CONFOUNDS / CAVEATS (honest):
  - Character is scored by a 7B model (an internal 7B instruct model, temp 0). Scorer noise attenuates coefficients
    toward zero (errors-in-variables); true effects are at least this large, not smaller.
  - Randomisation is WITHIN test only. It licenses the causal claim about headline character; it does
    NOT say the population is representative (Upworthy's 2013-15 progressive/viral audience).
  - Axes are correlated (affect/register/stance move together in clickbait), so single-axis credit is
    partly shared; read the multivariate betas as partial effects holding the other seven fixed.
  - CTR is bounded and small; a linear model is a local approximation. Impression-weighted and
    unweighted estimators agree in sign, which is the robustness that matters here.
  - The unit is the headline. Image was held fixed within test where the archive pairs them; residual
    headline/image coupling within a test is the main untestable residual confound.
